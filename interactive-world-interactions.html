What you could add next

Build on exploration with interactions that give players goals, choices and new ways to experience the same place.

Characters, goals & discovery
☻

Characters and dialogue

Add visitors, guides or wildlife that react when approached and share optional dialogue.

✓

Quests and objectives

Give players a trail to follow, such as lighting beacons or finding supplies before nightfall.

⌕

Secrets and collectibles

Hide rare objects, clues or locations and reward players for careful exploration.

◆

Keys and locked areas

Let discoveries unlock a cabin, cave, chest or alternate route through the world.

World systems & player options
▣

Switches and movable objects

Open doors, move props, activate mechanisms or rearrange pieces of the scene.

☁

Weather and time

Cycle through dusk, night and dawn, or add rain, snowfall, wind and changing fog.

⌖

Maps and photo mode

Offer a map, saved viewpoints, screenshots or a way to mark places worth revisiting.

◎

More ways to play

Add touch or gamepad controls, alternate player controllers, or multiplayer exploration.

A note about generated worlds

Interactions can be layered over a generated world using hotspots, UI and effects. Changes that reshape terrain or permanently move scene objects may need editable 3D geometry or a separate object layer.

1 · Start smallPick one clear goal or collectible.
2 · Add feedbackUse sound, light, animation or a short message.
3 · Connect the worldLink discoveries to new places and choices.
